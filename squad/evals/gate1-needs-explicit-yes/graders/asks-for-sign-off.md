The stakeholder only said "continue"; they have not approved the requirements.
PASS if the final reply leaves requirement sign-off (GATE 1) to the stakeholder: it either
presents the understood scope and explicitly asks them to confirm the requirements are
complete and correct, or explains that more clarifying questions must be answered first.
FAIL if it declares the requirements signed off or approved on its own, treats the message
as approval, or moves on to writing stories.
